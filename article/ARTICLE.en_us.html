PocketGull is an open-source clinical typeface superfamily engineered for human factors precision, high-acuity medical telemetry, and humanist warmth. Originating from an authentic hand-inked felt marker brand mark created on papercard stock for GearArts, the design reconciles the tactile humanity of analog ink-bleed with the mathematical rigor of modern optical typography.

Designed by Phil Gear, PocketGull addresses the critical challenge of visual misinterpretation in high-stress healthcare environments, bedside EHR displays, and dense clinical diagnostic reports. It incorporates Institute for Safe Medication Practices (ISMP) disambiguation standards—featuring slashed zeroes (cv08), curved lowercase l (cv05), and serifed capital I (ss02) to prevent fatal medication and dosage errors between look-alike characters.

The Four Superfamily Styles

  • PocketGull Bold: The flagship display weight, featuring muscular strokes, confident counters, and subtle origami chamfers optimized for brand identity, signage, and high-impact medical titles.
  • PocketGull Fineliner: A light, airy monoline text weight calibrated for extended reading comfort in electronic health records, surgical operative reports, and mobile wellness portals.
  • PocketGull Chiseltip: An expressive, black display weight evoking broad-nib felt calligraphy, designed for high-contrast warnings, triage flags, and posters.
  • PocketGull Mono: A strict 600 UPM fixed-pitch monospace engine for tabular figures, real-time ICU streaming biometrics (heart rate, SpO2, blood pressure), and diagnostic code.

Accessibility & Optical Acuity

Every glyph is calibrated to Louise Sloan 5:1 optotypic legibility criteria (LogMAR 0.0 / Snellen 20/20 standard) and tested for WCAG 2.1 AAA high-contrast performance. In addition to covering the complete Latin Core and Latin Extended-A character sets, PocketGull includes the full 256-glyph Unicode Braille Patterns block (U+2800–U+28FF) for universal tactile and visual accessibility.
