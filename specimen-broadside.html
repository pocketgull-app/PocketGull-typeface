The Living Typographic Charter

Typographic Respect & Harmonic Geometry Honoring Each Writing Tradition in Its Own Nature

• Let Latin be Latin: Crisp, disambiguated (ISMP), humanist, and readable.
• Let Arabic be Arabic: Let the nuqṭa keep its natural pen angle (the authentic reed-pen footprint).
• Let Braille be Braille: Preserve standard tactile cell geometries and negative space (ISO/TR 11548).
• Let Shorthand be Shorthand: Respect the phonemic size ratios of stenography without forcing it into Roman metal boxes.
• Keep the binary engine clean: Pad bytes to keep OTS happy, but don't confuse memory alignment with typographic beauty.

While Standard Raw ISO/TR 11548 provides the authoritative international specification for 8-dot Braille character identification and refreshable pins, mixed Latin-script typography requires optical grounding so tactile patterns sit harmoniously beside Latin letterforms without floating cell artifacts.
