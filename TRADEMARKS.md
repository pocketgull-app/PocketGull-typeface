# Trademark Policy & Brand Guidelines

**PocketGull Typeface Superfamily**  
*Open Source Licensing Coexistence & Lanham Act § 43(a) Policy*

---

## 🏛️ 1. Overview & Coexistence Principle

PocketGull Typeface Superfamily is dedicated to radical open-source typography. The font software itself is released under the **Apache License 2.0** with **Zero Reserved Font Names (No RFN)** to ensure frictionless upstream distribution across Google Fonts, Linux distributions, web browsers, and healthcare EHR platforms.

This document establishes the coexistence boundary between **Open Source Copyright Licensing (Apache 2.0)** and **Trademark Protection (Lanham Act § 43(a))**:

1. **The Font Code is Libre**: Anyone may freely download, embed, bundle, modify, and redistribute the font software binaries and vector sources pursuant to the terms of the SIL Apache 2.0.
2. **The Brand Identifiers are Protected**: The trade names, corporate marks, and commercial identifiers "PocketGull", "GearArts", and the PocketGull avian emblem are common law trademarks of **Phil Gear** and **PocketGull LLC**. They identify authentic, clinically vetted releases and proprietary healthcare software suites.

## 🛡️ 2. Permitted vs. Prohibited Uses

### Permitted Uses (No Prior Written Permission Required)
* **Standard OFL Distribution**: Distributing the original, unmodified font files (`PocketGull-Bold.ttf`, `PocketGull-Regular.woff2`, etc.) in open-source operating systems, software packages, webfont CDNs, and application bundles.
* **Document & Software Embedding**: Embedding PocketGull fonts in PDFs, EHR clinical interfaces, medical devices, electronic charts, books, signage, and websites.
* **Nominal & Truthful Referencing**: Using the name "PocketGull" truthfully to describe the font used (e.g., *"This clinical chart is set in PocketGull Bold"*).
* **Forking & Modifying Font Software**: Creating modified or derivative versions of the font code pursuant to SIL Apache 2.0. 

### Prohibited Uses (Requiring Explicit Authorization)
* **False Clinical Endorsement**: Using the name "PocketGull" or "GearArts" to market a commercial software product, telemedicine application, or medical hardware device in a manner that falsely implies endorsement, clinical certification, or affiliation with PocketGull LLC or Phil Gear.
* **Deceptive Trademark Registration**: Applying to register "PocketGull", "GearArts", or confusingly similar variations as a trademark, service mark, or domain name in the fields of typography, medical software, clinical decision support, or digital design.
* **Misleading Branding of Modified Forks**: While Apache 2.0 with No RFN permits retaining font names in derivative files, modifying the underlying character disambiguation (e.g., removing the ISMP slashed zero `cv08` or curved `l` `cv05`) while marketing the resulting software as *"Clinically Certified PocketGull"* constitutes false advertising and trademark infringement under Lanham Act § 43(a).
