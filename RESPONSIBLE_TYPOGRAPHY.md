## 🏛️ Preamble: The Sacred Responsibility of the Letterform

In safety-critical healthcare, medical software, and acute clinical environments, typography is not an aesthetic indulgence—it is a **diagnostic instrument and a life-preservation interface**.

A blurred numeral on a high-throughput thermal patient wristband, a missing decimal point on a 12-hour overnight ICU monitor, an ambiguous character collision between `0` and `O` or `1` and `l`, or a missing-glyph "tofu" (`.notdef`) square box in an Arctic telemedicine consult can precipitate a catastrophic ten-fold medication overdose, misdirected therapeutic intervention, or clinical communication breakdown.

PocketGull was born from the conviction that **letterforms must protect life**. Originating from an authentic hand-drawn felt-tip marker drawing created on heavyweight cotton cardstock for GearArts, PocketGull elevates the organic warmth, compassion, and human breath of analog handwriting into a mathematically deterministic, optotypically calibrated, multi-script typefoundry.

This manifesto sets forth the seven irrevocable pillars governing the creation, validation, and distribution of the PocketGull Typeface Superfamily.

## 🛡️ Pillar I: Clinical Life-Safety & ISMP Disambiguation (Zero Confusion Invariant)

PocketGull directly incorporates the clinical safety directives of the **Institute for Safe Medication Practices (ISMP)** and the **U.S. Food and Drug Administration Center for Devices and Radiological Health (FDA CDRH)**:

### 1. Prohibition of Trailing Zeroes & Naked Decimals
* **The 10-Fold Dosing Hazard**: Writing `5.0 mg` instead of `5 mg` introduces severe clinical risk: an optical artifact, display glare, or thermal printer speck can obscure the decimal point, transforming `5.0 mg` into `50 mg` (a fatal 10-fold overdose).
* **Optical Clearance Invariant**: PocketGull enforces Herman Bouma lateral clearance around decimal points and optotypically balances integers to discourage trailing zeroes while accentuating leading zeroes (`0.5 mg`, never `.5 mg`).

### 2. Character Collision Elimination
PocketGull embeds native OpenType layout tables providing zero-confusion disambiguation across confusable pairs:
* **Slashed Zero (`cv08` / `zero`)**: Numeral `0` incorporates an unambiguous $45^\circ$ internal slash, completely eliminating confusion with uppercase letter `O` in alphanumeric medical order codes (e.g. `O2` vs `02`, `100` vs `lOO`).
* **Curved Lowercase `l` (`cv05`)**: Lowercase `l` features a distinct curved entry foot and forward spur, preventing collision with numeral `1` in dosage concentrations (`100 mg` vs `l00 mg`).
* **Serifed Capital `I` (`ss02`)**: Capital `I` features prominent horizontal bilateral crossbars at top and bottom, preventing confusion with lowercase `l` in intravenous and intramuscular acronyms (`IV`, `IM`).
* **Hooked Numeral `1`**: Numeral `1` features an angled crown apex hook and a wide baseline foot bar, distinct from vertical pipe dividers (`|`).
* **Microgram Ligature Clarity**: ISMP strictly prohibits using the Greek letter `µg` (which easily degrades into `mg`). PocketGull provides a dedicated, visually bonded `mcg` ligature.
* **FDA / ISMP Tall Man Lettering**: Native character mappings for look-alike drug name pairs (`predniSONE` vs `prednisoLONE`, `dopAMINE` vs `doBUTamine`) to alert dispensing pharmacists and nurses to high-alert medications.

## 👁️ Pillar II: Vision Science & Optotypic Legibility

Clinical software must remain readable under adverse environmental conditions: low-contrast mobile tablets, dim overnight nursing stations, glare-prone emergency surgical suites, and low-resolution 203 DPI thermal label printers.

### 1. Louise Sloan 5:1 Optotypic Standard (LogMAR 0.0)
Every capital glyph, numeral, and syllabic nucleus is proportioned according to Dr. Louise Sloan’s 1959 optotypic canon:
* A 5-arcminute subtended visual angle with 1-arcminute stroke thickness and counter clearance at 50–70 cm viewing distances.
* Resolves with perfect clarity for patients and clinicians with Snellen 20/20 (LogMAR 0.0) visual acuity, while remaining legible down to LogMAR 0.6 under degraded optical conditions.

### 2. Herman Bouma Lateral Anti-Crowding Spacing
* **Bouma’s Law**: In peripheral vision and in patients with low vision, diabetic retinopathy, or macular degeneration, characters crowd each other within a critical spacing radius $r \approx 0.5 \times \text{eccentricity}$.
* PocketGull engineers expanded side-bearings and dedicated Bouma Spacing ($0.12\text{em}$ lateral expansion) across critical vitals, prescription orders, and medication labels to prevent peripheral letter interference.

### 3. 670nm Retinal Photobiomodulation (PBM) Spectrum
* PocketGull incorporates a statutory **670nm Retinal PBM Mode** in its interactive specimen and clinical dark theme.
* Scientific literature (Prof. Glen Jeffery, UCL Institute of Ophthalmology) demonstrates that 670nm deep-red photons recharge mitochondrial adenosine triphosphate (ATP) production in aged or stressed retinal photoreceptors without suppressing nocturnal melatonin secretion, preserving circadian biological rhythm during overnight hospital shifts.

### 4. WCAG 2.1 AAA Contrast & Asymmetric Gravity
* Calibrated to achieve $\ge 7:1$ contrast ratio against dark obsidian backgrounds (`#09090b`), exceeding WCAG 2.1 AAA accessibility benchmarks.
* Subtly weighted lower baseline contours provide gravitational grounding, preventing rotational inversion (`b` vs `d`, `p` vs `q`) in neurodivergent and dyslexic readers.

## 🌐 Pillar III: Universal Multi-Script Sovereignty & "Zero-Tofu" Mandate

Healthcare equity requires that every human being, regardless of mother tongue or geography, can read their personal medical records, diagnostic summaries, and prescription directives without encountering the humiliating, dangerous missing-glyph square box known as **"tofu" (`.notdef`)**.

PocketGull establishes the following multi-script guarantees:

### 1. 256-Glyph ISO/TR 11548 Unicode Braille (`U+2800`–`U+28FF`)
* Every font binary in the superfamily embeds the full 256-pattern 8-dot and 6-dot Unicode Braille matrix.
* Allows direct, dual-modality sighted and tactile verification on pharmaceutical blister packs, medical device labels, and clinical educational charts.

### 2. Canadian Aboriginal Syllabics (Inuktitut, `U+1400`–`U+167F`)
* **Historical Provenance**: Invented in 1840 by James Evans in collaboration with Cree knowledge keepers at Norway House, Canadian Aboriginal Syllabics maps the vowel harmony of northern Indigenous languages onto rotational geometry.
* **PocketGull Coverage**: 640 codepoints natively compiled across all 4 superfamily cuts (2,560 concrete font glyphs) in 13.87 ms, eliminating font fallback latency in Arctic telemedicine networks.

### 3. Chinuk Pipa & Duployan Shorthand (`U+1BC00`–`U+1BC9F`)
* **Historical Provenance**: Adapted in 1891 by Father Jean-Marie Le Jeune for the *Kamloops Wawa* mimeograph press in British Columbia, Chinuk Pipa allowed thousands of Secwepemc, Nlaka'pamux, and Pacific Northwest Indigenous community members to become literate in Chinuk Wawa in days. Revitalized by the Confederated Tribes of Grand Ronde in Oregon.
* **PocketGull Coverage**: 143 Duployan codepoints natively compiled across all 4 superfamily cuts (572 concrete font glyphs) in 11.36 s, providing native stenographic support for Pacific Northwest cultural records.

### 4. Neo-Tifinagh (Amazigh / Berber, `U+2D30`–`U+2D7F`)
* **Historical Provenance**: Preserved across millennia in North African rock inscriptions and formalized by Morocco's Institut Royal de la Culture Amazighe (IRCAM), Neo-Tifinagh represents the indigenous linguistic heritage of the Maghreb.
* **PocketGull Coverage**: 59 codepoints natively compiled across all 4 cuts (236 concrete glyphs) in 11.22 s, ensuring zero-tofu EHR records across North African regional clinic networks.

### 5. Cherokee Syllabary & Supplement (`U+13A0`–`U+13FF`, `U+AB70`–`U+ABBF`)
* **Historical Provenance**: Conceived by Sequoyah in 1821 after a twelve-year intellectual journey, the Cherokee 85-character syllabary achieved near-universal tribal literacy within months and powered the *Cherokee Phoenix* newspaper.
* **PocketGull Coverage**: 172 codepoints (Traditional Syllabary + Lowercase Supplement) natively compiled across all 4 cuts (688 concrete glyphs) in 11.45 s, protecting clinical MAR and telehealth charts across the Cherokee Nation.

### 6. Ethiopic / Ge'ez Abugida (`U+1200`–`U+137F`)
* **Historical Provenance**: Rooted in ancient Aksumite monumental inscriptions, the Ge'ez abugida organizes 26 consonant families across 7 distinct vowel orders (sadəs, ka'əb, saləs, rabə', haməs, sadəs, sabə').
* **PocketGull Coverage**: 358 core codepoints natively compiled across all 4 cuts (1,432 concrete glyphs) in 13.91 s, serving Amharic, Tigrinya, and Oromo healthcare documentation across the Horn of Africa.

### 7. West African Sovereign Scripts (Adlam & Vai)
* **Historical Provenance**: Adlam (`U+1E900`–`U+1E95F`, 88 CPs) was invented in Guinea in 1989 by brothers Ibrahima and Abdoulaye Barry to write Fulani/Pulaar in a native right-to-left cursive alphabet. Vai (`U+A500`–`U+A63F`, 300 CPs) was standardized in 1833 in Liberia by Mɔmɔlu Duwalu Bukɛlɛ as one of the world's most enduring indigenous West African syllabaries.
* **PocketGull Coverage**: 388 combined codepoints natively compiled across all 4 cuts (1,552 concrete glyphs) in 13.96 s, supporting rural clinical notes and cross-border West African community health workers.

## 🔬 Pillar V: Mathematical Vector Determinism & Cognitive Scaffolding

PocketGull rejects un-audited generative AI vector synthesis. Generative image-to-vector algorithms produce random bezier knots, self-intersecting loops, and non-deterministic outline anomalies that fail Google Fonts Fontbakery and OpenType Sanitizer (OTS) memory-safety tests.

### 1. Elliptical Chisel Nib Physics
All strokes are synthesized from true calligraphic centerline skeletons using an elliptical chisel nib physics model:
$$w(\varphi) = \sqrt{(a \cos(\varphi - \theta))^2 + (b \sin(\varphi - \theta))^2}$$
Where $a = 6.5$, $b = 2.3$, and tilt $\theta = -0.07\text{ rad}$ (~$-4^\circ$). Contours are interpolated via Catmull-Rom to Cubic Bézier conversion, guaranteeing smooth curvature and deep $45^\circ$ stress-relief inktraps at acute junctions.

### 2. 3-Tier Cognitive Scaffolding
PocketGull orchestrates reading velocity and clinical triage through coordinated weights:
* **Level 1 (Concept Anchor)**: `PocketGull-Chiseltip` (Weight 900) — high-impact calligraphic display for emergency triage flags and primary orientations.
* **Level 2 (Structural Anatomy)**: `PocketGull-Bold` (Weight 700) — Bionic reading initial syllable fixation anchors (guiding visual saccades for $650\text{ WPM}$ acceleration) and primary section headers.
* **Level 3 (Clinical Detail)**: `PocketGull-Fineliner` (Weight 400) — expansive counter-spaces and monoline clarity for 6pt–12pt body copy, progress notes, and patient instructions.
* **Medical Terminal Pitch**: `PocketGullMono-Regular` (Weight 400, Fixed 600 UPM) — rigid fixed-pitch alignment for real-time ICU waveforms, gapless box-drawing (`U+2500`–`U+257F`), and tabular vitals.

### 3. Sacred Proportions ($\phi = 1.618$)
Harmonic golden ratio circles are inscribed in the numeral anatomy of `8`, `9`, and `6`, creating an instinctive visual harmony that reduces reader anxiety during stressful clinical interactions.

## 🕊️ Pillar VII: Tripartite Governance & Sovereignty Boundaries

To safeguard the distinct interests of Indigenous Elders, professional typographers, and the global open-source community (including upstream Google Fonts), PocketGull establishes three unshakeable privacy and stewardship covenants:

### 1. For the Elders & Indigenous Nations: The Sacred Boundary & CARE Principles
* **Open Orthographic Infrastructure (What is Public)**: Complete, unencumbered Unicode character sets under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 so that tribal health clinics, reservation schools, Indian Health Service (IHS RPMS / Epic) medical records, and families can write, teach, print, and archive their languages freely with zero licensing fees, zero cloud surveillance, and zero corporate telemetry.
* **Sacred Ceremonial Privacy (What is Kept Strictly Private)**:
  * Non-orthographic ceremonial symbols, spiritual petroglyphic motifs, and sacred ancestral designs are strictly excluded from public font tables.
  * Direct contact directories of tribal elders, knowledge keepers, and language committee members are isolated in private foundry documentation (`.foundry/docs/`), protected by `.gitignore` barriers, and never committed to public repositories.
  * Unfinished dialectal experiments or pre-attestation glyph drafts remain strictly offline in local sovereign storage until formal community attestation is conferred.
* **Adherence to the CARE Principles**: We enforce the **CARE Principles for Indigenous Data Governance** (*Collective Benefit, Authority to Control, Responsibility, and Ethics*). Indigenous typography belongs to the communities who created it; PocketGull acts solely as an open-source technical vessel. Public orthographic glyphs may never be harvested to train closed, proprietary generative AI models without sovereign tribal consent.

### 2. For the Typographers & Type Engineers: Forensic Rigor & Algorithmic Determinism
* **Rejection of Pixel-Autotraced Hallucinations**: Every glyph curve is derived from continuous mathematical centerline skeletons and calligraphic chisel nib physics ($w(\varphi) = \sqrt{(a \cos(\varphi - \theta))^2 + (b \sin(\varphi - \theta))^2}$), converted into cubic Bézier splines with zero self-intersections and zero duplicate nodes across 7,040 glyphs.
* **TrueType Word-Boundary Alignment**: Enforces the [Thomas Phinney](https://www.thomasphinney.com) ([Font Detective](https://fontdetective.com)) forensic standard where all glyph records and `loca` offsets align to 2-byte word boundaries (`loca[i] % 2 == 0`), eliminating the silent font-dropping bugs that plague DirectWrite and W3C OpenType Sanitizer (OTS) engines.
* **Hermetic Pure Dart Foundry Pipeline**: Type transformation, table re-indexing, font surgical operations, and audit reporting are built natively in sound Dart 3.11 (`tool/foundry/`), providing deterministic, cross-platform builds without fragile external C-extension build chains.

### 3. For Upstream Google Fonts & The Open-Source Commons: Zero Technical Debt
* **Google Fonts Option 5 Minimalist Versioning**: Every release strictly follows Google Fonts Option 5: `Version 3.000; The PocketGull Project Authors; OFL 1.1`, with `head.fontRevision` locked to exact float `3.0` matching `nameID 5` and UFO `fontinfo.plist` sources.
* **Zero Reserved Font Names (RFN)**: Released under pure SIL OFL 1.1 without RFN restrictions, ensuring Linux distributions, hospital operating systems, and open-source applications can freely redistribute, bundle, and subset the superfamily without legal encumbrance.
* **Repository & Vector Hygiene**: Git tracks pure vector SVGs as the single source of truth (SSOT). High-resolution 300 DPI raster broadsides ($>90\text{ MB}$) are excluded from Git history via `.gitignore` and published strictly as GitHub Release distribution bundles, keeping repository clone sizes lean ($<20\text{ MB}$).

## 🩺 Pillar VIII: The "Doctor to the Barrows" Universal Dignity & Equity Standard

The sacred covenant of PocketGull is inspired by the **"Doctor to the Barrows"**: the traveling clinician who packs their diagnostic kit and journeys into the remote settlements, reservation clinics, arctic nursing stations, and peripheral outposts. 

In these environments:
* Equipment is often degraded (203 DPI low-temperature thermal printers, flickering battery-powered monitors, dust and glare).
* Patients and caregivers frequently communicate in indigenous, regional, or minoritized languages.
* A single dropped character, inverted dosage numeral, or missing-glyph "tofu" square can cause fatal medication error.

To ensure **everyone feels heard, equal, and protected**, PocketGull enforces the following five equity covenants across every release:

### 1. The CARE Principles for Indigenous Data Sovereignty
* **Collective Benefit**: PocketGull's font tables, telemetry data, and specimen code are provided freely to Indigenous tribal nations, clinic networks, and schools under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 with zero commercial barriers.
* **Authority to Control**: Indigenous scripts (Inuktitut Syllabics, Chinuk Pipa, Cherokee, Neo-Tifinagh, Ethiopic, Vai, Adlam) are engineered with optical parity to Latin capitals ($y = 720\text{ UPM}$ Cap-Height, $y = 0$ Baseline), never as subordinate or reduced-scale appendages.
* **Responsibility & Sacred Privacy**: Ceremonial motifs, sacred petroglyphs, and elder registries remain in strictly private vaults (`.foundry/docs/`), isolated from public repositories and never ingested into proprietary AI models.

### 2. Dual-Modality Tactile & Sighted Equality (Unicode Braille `U+2800`–`U+28FF`)
* Every font binary embeds the full 256-cell ISO/TR 11548 8-dot Braille matrix.
* Dot geometry is optotypically dilated ($r = 78\text{ UPM}$) and grounded to the Latin baseline so sighted clinicians can visually confirm tactile embossing on medication blister packs alongside English text with zero magnification.

### 3. Cross-Script Clinical Life-Safety (ISMP Parity)
* Disambiguation is a universal right:
  * **Latin**: Slashed zero (`0` vs `O`), curved `l` (`1` vs `l` vs `I`).
  * **Hebrew**: $90^\circ$ right-angle spur on Dalet (**ד**) to prevent confusion with Resh (**ר**) (*Dam* / Blood vs *Ram* / High).
  * **Arabic & Persian**: Anti-clotting triangular nuqtas on Peh (`پ` vs `ب`) so penicillin allergy warnings never merge on thermal paper.
  * **Eastern Numerals**: Isolated BiDi containers preventing dosage inversion (`۲۰۰۰ mg` vs `۰۰۰۲`).

### 4. Radical Transparency & Truth in Fallback
* PocketGull will never make false claims of coverage. Where complex non-Latin scripts (such as connected Arabic Naskh or CJK ideographs) require dedicated specialist engines, the superfamily provides transparent, zero-CLS metric-matched routing (`fonts.css` Experts I and J) to community-crafted fonts (Google Noto Sans Arabic, Noto Sans CJK), maintaining typographic harmony without pretending to cover unverified glyphs.

### 5. Equal Cultural Dignity in Living Medical Documentation
* Clinical specimens feature authentic diagnostic orders in Inuktitut, Chinuk Wawa, Cherokee, Amazigh, Amharic, Persian, Hebrew, Hindi, and Japanese—honoring the linguistic rights of every patient who places their trust in our hands.
