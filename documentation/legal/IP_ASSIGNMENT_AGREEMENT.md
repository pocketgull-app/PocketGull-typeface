# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This **Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement** (the "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of **June 15, 2026** (the "Effective Date"), by and between:

* **ASSIGNOR**: **Phillip Gear**, an individual residing at 101 SW Madison St #1664, Portland, Oregon 97207 ("Assignor"); and
* **ASSIGNEE**: **PocketGull LLC**, an Oregon limited liability company, with its principal place of business at 101 SW Madison St #1664, Portland, Oregon 97207, Oregon Secretary of State Registry No. 258869891, EIN 42-3162850 ("Assignee" or the "Company").

---

### RECITALS

**WHEREAS**, Assignor is the sole author, developer, and legal owner of certain proprietary and open-source intellectual property, including typeface software, font binaries, vector artwork, compilation toolchains, domain names, and trademarks associated with **PocketGull** and **GearArts**; and

**WHEREAS**, Assignor has formed Assignee under the Oregon Limited Liability Company Act and desires to transfer and assign all right, title, and interest in and to said intellectual property to Assignee as an initial capital contribution in exchange for one hundred percent (100%) of the Membership Interests in the Company pursuant to the Company's Operating Agreement; and

**WHEREAS**, Assignee desires to acquire all such right, title, and interest from Assignor upon the terms and conditions set forth herein.

---

### AGREEMENT

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and the issuance of one hundred percent (100%) Membership Interest in the Company, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged pursuant to Internal Revenue Code § 721, the parties agree as follows:

#### 1. Assignment of Intellectual Property
Assignor hereby irrevocably sells, assigns, transfers, grants, and conveys to Assignee, its successors, and assigns, forever, all of Assignor’s worldwide right, title, and interest in and to the following (collectively, the "Assigned Property"):
* **Trademarks & Brand Assets**: All trademarks, service marks, trade names, logos, slogans, and wordmarks for **"PocketGull"**, **"GearArts"**, and the PocketGull avian insignia, whether registered or arising under common law, together with the goodwill of the business symbolized thereby and all rights to sue for past, present, or future infringement.
* **Domain Names & Accounts**: All rights, title, and administrative control over the domain names `pocketgull.app` and `font.pocketgull.app`, the GitHub organization `pocketgull-app`, the Zenodo archive DOI allocations, and associated cloud/web infrastructure.
* **Copyrights & Font Software**: All original works of authorship, copyrights, and copyright applications/registrations in the **PocketGull Typeface Superfamily**, including without limitation TrueType (`.ttf`) binaries, Web Open Font Format (`.woff2`) binaries, Unified Font Object (`UFO3`) sources, Glyphs sources, vector SVG plates, documentation, and all associated build engines (`pocketgull_foundry.dart`, `foundry_spector.dart`).
* **Clinical & Telemetry Designs**: All proprietary typographic formulas, Louise Sloan 5:1 optotypic geometries, ISMP character disambiguation layouts, sub-cell ICU waveforms, and related technical specifications.

#### 2. Open Source Licensing Covenant
Assignee acknowledges that portions of the Assigned Property have been released under the **SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL-1.1)** with Zero Reserved Font Names. Assignee succeeds to all rights of the Licensor and copyright holder under said license, including the exclusive right to enforce license compliance, maintain official upstream distributions, and govern the project repository.

#### 3. Representations and Warranties
Assignor represents and warrants that: (a) Assignor is the sole creator and exclusive lawful owner of all right, title, and interest in the Assigned Property; (b) the Assigned Property is free and clear of all liens, claims, encumbrances, or security interests; (c) Assignor has full legal power and authority to enter into and execute this Agreement; and (d) the execution and delivery of this Agreement does not conflict with or violate any other agreement to which Assignor is a party.

#### 4. Further Assurances
Assignor agrees to perform all further acts and execute, acknowledge, and deliver any documents, instruments, assignments, or transfer powers that Assignee may reasonably request to record, register, perfect, or enforce Assignee's full legal and equitable ownership of the Assigned Property with the United States Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO), the United States Copyright Office, ICANN-accredited domain registrars, or any other government registry.

#### 5. Governing Law and Severability
This Agreement shall be construed, interpreted, and governed by the laws of the **State of Oregon**, without regard to conflict of law principles. If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.
