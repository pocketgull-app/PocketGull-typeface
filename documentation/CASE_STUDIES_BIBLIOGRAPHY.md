## 🧭 Executive Overview

This master bibliography compiles the foundational peer-reviewed literature, historical manuscripts, Indigenous knowledge syntheses, pharmacological monographs, vision science trials, and international computing standards underpinning the **7 Canonical Case Studies** of the PocketGull Typefoundry:

1. **Case Study 01**: Inuktitut Syllabics (*Canadian Aboriginal Syllabics, Arctic Clinical Telehealth & Inuit Qaujimajatuqangit*)
