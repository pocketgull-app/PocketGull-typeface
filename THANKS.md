# 🙏 Acknowledgements & Thanks

The PocketGull Typeface Superfamily is an open-source clinical, optotypic, and sovereign multi-script typography initiative dedicated to patient safety, health equity, and world language preservation.

This work stands upon the shoulders of brilliant software engineers, linguistic scholars, clinical researchers, and open-source advocates. We express our deepest appreciation and gratitude to:

## 🎯 The Dart Programming Language & Google Engineering

We extend our sincere thanks to **The Dart Programming Language Project** ([dart.dev](https://dart.dev)), the **Google Dart & Flutter Engineering Teams**, and the broader Dart open-source ecosystem.

Dart serves as the primary language for PocketGull's standalone type engineering, procedural vector geometry synthesis, phonological data matrices, and automated pre-flight testing suites:
- **Sound Static Typing & Null Safety**: Enabled defect-free algorithmic coordinate transforms and bezier spline dilation across thousands of glyphs.
- **Dart 3 Pattern Matching & Exhaustive Sealed Hierarchies**: Streamlined the classification of phonological matrices, diacritical accents, and vowel harmony rotations.
- **Batteries-Included Tooling**: Zero virtualenv friction, zero configuration overhead, microsecond-accurate benchmarking (`Stopwatch`), and instant execution via `dart run`.
- **Predictable Concurrency**: Pure single-threaded event-loop architecture that ensures deterministic batch specimen rendering without thread starvation or race conditions.
