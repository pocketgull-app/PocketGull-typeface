PocketGull Mono is a fixed-pitch clinical telemetry and developer monospace typeface engineered by Phil Gear.

Locked to a strict 600 UPM advance width, PocketGull Mono is tailored for ICU patient monitoring displays, digital telemetry dashboards, laboratory instrumentation, and code editing environments. It synthesizes tactile humanist warmth with Louise Sloan 5:1 optotypes, full 256-glyph ISO tactile Braille, sub-cell ECG waveforms, and Institute for Safe Medication Practices (ISMP) character disambiguation (mandatory slashed zero, curved lowercase l, serifed capital I, and crossbarred Z) to eliminate human misinterpretation in life-critical healthcare and high-density telemetry environments.

To contribute, see github.com/pocketgull-app/pocketgull-typeface.
