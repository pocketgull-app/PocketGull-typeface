📖 How to Implement PocketGull in Your Project

Follow these simple steps to integrate PocketGull into HTML/CSS, React, Next.js, or Vue.

1
Link the Stylesheet
Add <link rel="stylesheet" href="https://typeface.pocketgull.app/pocketgull.css"> to your HTML head or self-host the /fonts/ directory.
2
Apply Utility Classes
Use .font-pocketgull-heading for titles, .font-pocketgull-body for text, and .font-pocketgull-telemetry for data.
3
Configure Tailwind CSS
Add fontFamily: { pocketgull: ['PocketGull', 'sans-serif'], pocketgullMono: ['PocketGull Mono', 'monospace'] } in tailwind.config.js.
4
Enable Patient Safety
Add .clinical-ismp-safe to medical dosages and telemetry feeds to ensure zero numeral/letter ambiguity.

Patient Safety Standards

ISMP / FDA Glyph Disambiguation

Engineered to eliminate look-alike medication dosage and telemetry errors in critical care and emergency EHR workflows.

Numeral Zero vs Alpha O cv08 • Slashed Zero
0  O  o

Internal 45° optical slash prevents confusion between numeric dosage values (e.g. 50 mg) and uppercase oxygen notation (5 O₂).

Numeral 1 vs Lower l vs Upper I cv05 + ss02
1  l  I  |

Distinct hooked top serif on numeral 1, curved bottom exit stroke on lowercase l, and bilateral top/bottom slabs on capital I.

Numeral 5 vs Capital S Aperture Geometry
5  S  s

Generous terminal angles and squared shoulder joints prevent confusion on low-resolution bedside monitors.

Numeral 8 vs Capital B Waist Proportions
8  B  β

Asymmetric upper/lower loop ratio with distinct vertical stem on capital B preserves optical differentiation.
