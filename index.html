Typographic Principles

Multi-Script Typographic Principles Respecting Each Writing Tradition's Native Structure

• Let Latin be Latin: Crisp, disambiguated (ISMP), humanist, and readable.
• Let Arabic be Arabic: Honor the authentic reed-pen angle and baseline flow rather than artificial geometric flattening.
• Let Braille be Braille: Preserve standard tactile cell geometries and negative space (ISO/TR 11548 & ISO 17049).
• Let Canadian Syllabics be Syllabics: Respect rotational symmetry, optical stroke weights, and stroke terminals.
• Let Stenography be Stenography: Respect phonemic scale ratios without forcing shorthand into Roman letter proportions.
• Keep the binary engine clean: Pad bytes to keep OTS happy (loca[i] % 2 == 0), ensuring 100% memory safety across all operating systems.
Foundry Compliance & Standards

The Nine Architectural Pillars W3C OTS & TrueType Invariants

1. 1000 UPM Em-Square

Conforms to ISO/IEC 14496-22 with fsSelection bit 7 enabled across all cuts.

2. 2-Byte Word Invariant

Every glyph padded to even bytes; loca[i] % 2 == 0 for 100% OTS memory safety.

3. Bit-7 Flag Masking

Reserved bit 7 in TTGlyphPen flags strictly zeroed (flag & 0x3F) preventing driver crashes.

4. ISMP Disambiguation

Slashed zero (cv08), curved l (cv05), serifed I (ss02), slashed Z (cv11) for clinical safety.

5. ISO/TR 11548 Braille

Full 256 Unicode Braille (U+2800–U+28FF) tactile 8-dot geometry across all weights.

6. Monospace 600 UPM

PocketGull Mono strictly 600 UPM fixed pitch (isFixedPitch = 1, panose.bProportion = 9).

7. DirectWrite ClearType

Version 1 gasp table mapped to symmetric smoothing and grayscale antialiasing.

8. Baseline & x-Height Lock

Non-Latin scripts optically grounded to Latin baseline (y=0) and x-height (y=540 UPM).

9. Zero-CLS Metric Overrides

Enforces ascent-override: 96%, descent-override: 24%, and size-adjust: 100% on system fallbacks for 0px layout shift.
